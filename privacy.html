DATA PRIVACY & GDPR

Privacy Policy

隱私權保護政策 • Last Updated: November 2026

At Happiness Table™, we respect your personal privacy as a core value. We strictly adhere to data minimization principles under GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection standards.

🔒 Zero-Cookie & No-Tracker Pledge (零追蹤 Cookie 承諾)
We do not use invasive third-party ad pixels, Facebook tracking tags, or intrusive advertising cookies. We only collect the bare minimum personal data required to deliver your digital purchases and provide customer support.

1. Information We Collect (我們收集的資訊)

We only collect personal information when you explicitly provide it to us:

  • Order Fulfillment Information: Name, Email address, and Country/Region (used exclusively for delivering digital download links, issuing license keys, and sending purchase receipts).
  • Support Inquiries: The correspondence and details you submit when emailing our customer support desk.

2. Payment Information & Merchant of Record (Paddle)

We do NOT collect, view, or store your credit card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account credentials. All transactions are securely routed through our authorized Merchant of Record, Paddle.com Market Ltd.

Paddle maintains strict PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance and processes all financial transactions under rigorous international encryption standards. For more information, please review Paddle's Privacy Policy.

3. How We Use Your Data (資料使用範疇)

Your personal data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Immediate electronic delivery of your digital products and course access.
  • Validating your 14-Day Money Back Guarantee and processing refunds.
  • Providing technical support and answering your creative inquiries.
  • Sending essential service notifications (such as critical toolkit updates or webinar schedules).

We will never sell, rent, trade, or distribute your email address or personal details to third-party marketers or advertisers.

4. Data Retention (資料保存原則)

We retain your purchase records only as long as necessary to maintain your digital access, support your inquiries, and satisfy tax/accounting regulations required by our merchant partners.

5. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Rights)

Regardless of your geographic location, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update or correct your contact information.
  • Deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request that we delete your customer profile and email records from our active fulfillment database.

6. How to Contact Us for Data Inquiries

To exercise any of your privacy rights, request data deletion, or ask questions about our data practices, please email our Privacy Officer directly:

  • Responsible Representative: Alex Chien (簡家旗)
  • Response SLA: Within 48 hours
